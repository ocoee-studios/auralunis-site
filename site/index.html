BEFORE YOU LOOK UP

A few answers.

Do I need a telescope?

No. Start with your iPhone, a view of the sky, and a little curiosity. AuraLunis helps you explore celestial objects and learn what you’re seeing.

Does Sky Lens use my camera?

No. Sky Lens is a rendered planetarium that aligns with your direction using location, compass, and device-motion data.

Is AuraLunis free?

AuraLunis is free to download with core sky and learning experiences. An optional Lifetime purchase unlocks the complete premium experience. Check the App Store for current pricing in your region.

Can I save what I discover?

AuraLunis includes a Vault for celestial discoveries, notes, and captures, along with tools to share your sky. Feature access varies between free and Lifetime.

How can I get help?

Email admin@ocoeestudios.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of what you need help with.
